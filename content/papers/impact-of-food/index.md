##### Abstract

Automated coaching based on accurate glucose predictions can help improve the self-management of diabetes. In this study, we built a transformer based large health model (LHM) to study the impact of food on glucose prediction at 30 minutes, 60 minutes, and 2-hour intervals. The LHM takes glucose and other covariates such as food as inputs to predict future glucose levels.
